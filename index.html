Weather, tides and sea safety

Forecasts are guidance, not a guarantee of safe conditions, and may change quickly. Check official forecasts, wind gusts, wave conditions, tide times and local warnings before setting out. Strong tidal streams can be dangerous, especially around headlands and narrow channels. On a boat, wear a suitable lifejacket and check the vessel and safety equipment. From shore, keep clear of breaking waves and slippery edges, and never risk being cut off by the tide. If you are in immediate danger at the coast, call 999 and ask for the Coastguard.
